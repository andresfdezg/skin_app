## What we can do
- Use, copy, change and share the images and metadata, including in trained models, figures and derived datasets.
- Do all of this only for **non-commercial** purposes, such as research and teaching. We can't sell the data, or anything built mainly from it, or use it mainly for commercial gain.

## What we must do
1. **Cite the source** anywhere we use or share the data: code, reports, papers and presentations.
2. If we share the data or a modified version of it:
   - name the creators,
   - state the license (CC BY-NC 4.0) and link to it,
   - link to the original dataset,
   - say whether we changed it (for example resized, cropped or filtered images) and how.
3. Don't place extra restrictions on anyone who receives the data from us. Anything we share must stay usable under the same terms.
4. Don't suggest that the dataset authors endorse or sponsor our project.

## What we should keep in mind
- The data comes **"as-is"**, with no warranty of accuracy or fitness for any purpose. The authors are not liable for how we use it. Nothing built in this project is a clinical tool.
- If we break these terms, our right to use the data ends automatically. It comes back if we fix the problem within 30 days of finding it.
